Frequently asked questions

How do I install the theme?

Upload the IRONCLAD theme package in Shopify admin under Online Store → Themes, then click Customize to begin setting up the theme.

How do I customize colors?

Open the Shopify Theme Editor and use the Colors & Fonts settings. IRONCLAD includes branded dark/light mode control and editable palette options.

Is the theme mobile responsive?

Yes. IRONCLAD is built mobile-first with fully responsive layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

How do I set up B2B features?

B2B features work best with Shopify Plus or apps for wholesale pricing. Use IRONCLAD’s pricing and customer group settings, then customize checkout options in your admin.

Need direct support?

For complex customizations or theme installation help, email themes@inlowprice.com and include your Shopify store URL.
